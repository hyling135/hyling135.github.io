I am a third-year PhD student in the [Programming Methodology Group](https://www.pm.inf.ethz.ch/) at ETH Zürich, supervised by [Prof. Dr. Peter Müller](https://www.pm.inf.ethz.ch/people/personal/pmueller-pers.html). My research interests are theoretical foundations for program verification, in particular program logics and verification of probabilistic distributed systems.

Before I started my PhD, I obtained my master's degree in computer science at ETH Zürich, and my bachelor's degree in computer science and technology at Peking University, during which I did research on blockchain theory and algorithmic game theory (AGT) at the [Center on Frontiers of Computing Studies (CFCS)](https://cfcs.pku.edu.cn/english/) under the supervision of [Prof. Xiaotie Deng](https://cfcs.pku.edu.cn/english/people/faculty/xiaotiedeng/index.htm).
